Someone you can be completely yourself around. Someone who listens when you need to talk, holds you when you need comfort, laughs with you when you're being your adorable little self, and gives you the space and understanding you deserve when things get difficult. I want my love to feel like a place where your heart can rest. ❤️🫂

I want us to grow, to communicate, to understand each other even when we're upset, and to remember that we're supposed to be on the same team. I want us to learn how to disagree without hurting each other, how to apologise sincerely, and how to make our relationship a place of peace, respect, softness, and happiness. Because, my love, you deserve to be loved gently, and so do I. And I want us to keep choosing that kind of love, together. ❤️

I want my actions to make you feel the love that my words are trying to explain today.

And if I could go back to the beginning and meet you all over again, knowing everything I know now — every laugh, every tear, every beautiful memory, every difficult lesson, and every little moment that has made us who we are — I would still want the chance to know you, to fall for you, and to discover our story. Not because every part has been easy, but because what we've shared has meant so much to me, and because I believe we can keep making our love healthier, stronger, and more beautiful.

Thank you, my baby, for being a part of these four years of my life. Thank you for the memories, the laughter, the affection, the moments that made me smile when I needed them, and all the little pieces of our story that I will always remember. Thank you for being someone who means so much to me, someone whose happiness matters to me, and someone I still want to make new memories with.

And listen, meri babbiiee, I still have SO MUCH love to give you. 😭❤️ Four years and I still want to flirt with you, tease you, make you blush, make you laugh, hold your face in my hands, kiss your forehead, and remind you that you're my girl. And you're still the person I want to make feel special, not just on our anniversary, but on all the random, ordinary days when there isn't any special occasion at all.

I hope that when you look back on these four years, you remember not only how much we've loved each other, but also how much we've learned. And I hope the years ahead bring us more peace, more understanding, more patience, more trust, more affection, and so many reasons to smile together. I hope we become better at loving each other, not just more attached to each other. I hope we protect what we have by treating each other with the care and respect that our love deserves.

Happy fourth anniversary, meri jaan. ❤️🧿

Four years of us. Four years of a story that means more to me than I can fit into one letter. Four years of having you as someone so special in my heart, and a future full of little moments I still hope to share with you.

I love you, my bebbuu. I love you, my babbiiee. I love you, meri buttkkii. I love you in all the silly little ways, all the soft and romantic ways, and all the serious ways that words sometimes struggle to explain.

Here's to our four years, my love, and to the beautiful memories we have yet to create. May we keep growing, keep laughing, keep being a little crazy together, and keep finding new reasons to fall in love with each other — while never forgetting to be gentle with each other's hearts.

You're my baby, my favourite person, and a very special part of my story. Today, tomorrow, and in all the little moments in between. ♾️❤️
